Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

Overview

Giraffe Cave LLC ("we," "us," or "our") built the Show Up app as a personal development tool. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Show Up stores your daily promises and check-in results locally on your device. Your progress is also automatically and anonymously backed up to our secure cloud servers to protect your data and allow you to restore it on a new device.

All backed-up entries are encrypted on your device before transmission using AES encryption. Your unique anonymous ID serves as the encryption key — this means even we cannot read the content of your promises. No personally identifiable information is ever collected or transmitted.

A randomly generated anonymous ID (UUID) is created on your device at first install. This ID is not linked to your name, email address, or any other identifying information. It is used solely to associate your encrypted backup with your device.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any personally identifiable information unless you contact us directly for support. We do not use advertising networks or sell data to third parties. We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites.

Data Deletion

Your data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or use the "Start Fresh" feature in Settings. To request deletion of your cloud backup data, email us at showup@giraffecave.com with your Backup ID (found in Settings → Cloud Backup). We will delete your data within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

Show Up uses RevenueCat to process in-app purchases and Supabase for secure encrypted cloud backup storage. We do not share your promise content with any third party. RevenueCat Privacy Policy · Supabase Privacy Policy

Crisis Safety

Show Up is a self-reflection tool, not a mental health treatment or crisis intervention service. If certain keywords suggesting distress are detected in the promise input field, the app surfaces crisis support resources including the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. This detection occurs entirely on your device and no content is transmitted to us.

Children's Privacy

Show Up is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
